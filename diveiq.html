DiveIQ

Personal dive log and weight guidance for Android

What is DiveIQ?

DiveIQ helps you log dives and get practical weight recommendations based on your body profile, gear, water type, and how you felt in the water. It is designed as a single-user app: one diver profile on your device, with data stored locally unless you choose future cloud features.

Features

  • Create your diver profile (weight, height, certification, default gear)
  • Log dives with depth, duration, pressures, and buoyancy feedback
  • See recommended lead weight before and after each dive
  • Personal offset learning from valid dives over time
  • Dive history and dive details on your phone
  • Settings to update your profile and preferences

Availability

DiveIQ is distributed on Google Play under the package name com.em_projects.diveiq. Release timing and regions are listed on the store listing when published.
